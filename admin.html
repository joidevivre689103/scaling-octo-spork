Engagement Over Time — all users combined

Active time is summed across users, so it exceeds wall-clock time once more than one person is reading at once. Active Time is the engaged figure — heartbeats counted only while the tab was visible and interacted with in the previous 180s, so a tab parked in the background accrues none of it. Span is the raw session window, idle gaps and all, up to the 30-minute timeout; it is shown as context, and a Span far larger than the Active Time is tab-parking rather than reading. Page Views counts everyone, Users counts only signed-in readers — the anonymous column shows the gap, and Views/User divides signed-in views by signed-in readers so the two sides of that ratio match. Unique users cannot be added across days without double-counting returning readers, so month and all-time rows show the largest single day as a floor, marked “≥”.

Close a reader account

Deletes users/{uid}, subscriptions/{emailKey}, the reader's logins records and oracleScores (which removes their past-edition leaderboard entries), then revokes their tokens and deletes the Firebase Auth record. Three of those stores are keyed by email rather than uid, which is why this runs server-side rather than as a loop over the uid.

Redacts rather than deletes: their usedPseudonyms claim keeps the name reserved but loses the identifier on it; and any comments or btsReplies left standing are de-identified whether or not you tick the box below — a contribution can survive without carrying an address.

Not touched: newsletter subscriptions (they live in MailerLite — unsubscribe there separately if asked), payment and tax records, and published contributor articles. A closure record is written and kept for six years.

Required. The time the reader confirmed the closure from the address on the account — this is the evidence the closure record carries. Write to them and use the time of their reply.
Otherwise a live or past-due subscription blocks the closure — cancel it in Stripe first.

Closure log

Retained six years from closure, then swept automatically. Purpose-quarantined: not a mailing list, not a suppression list, never consulted when a new account is opened.

Site Access

Who can see the site right now
How tiers work: Site visitors must be at or above this tier to bypass the coming-soon page. Closed means nobody (only the owner-bypass URL works). Beta is the right pre-launch setting — only users assigned the beta, admin, or superadmin role in the User Analytics table get in. Public opens the site to everyone — set this on launch day. Changes take effect within ~5 minutes (session cache TTL).

Podcast feed configuration

What appears on Apple, Spotify and other podcast apps
How the feed works: the latest cap episodes appear in the public RSS feed (/podcast/rss.xml). Older episodes age off the public feed and live on the paywalled audio.html. Cap = 6 gives roughly six weeks of weekly content as a public sample — tune up if platform reach is poor, down if subscriber conversion is poor. The exclude switch is the future paid-podcast lever: flip it the day audio-only standalones become a subscriber benefit (e.g. Apple Podcasts Subscriptions). Changes take effect within ~10 minutes (CDN cache).
